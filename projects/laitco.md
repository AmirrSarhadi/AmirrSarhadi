## Frontend & UX Focus

### Persian-first RTL design

The interface was developed around Persian content rather than treating RTL as an afterthought.

That includes:

- RTL page structure
- Persian typography
- Correct alignment and spacing
- Mobile-friendly text hierarchy
- Persian-only presentation where mixed-language content reduced visual quality

### Responsive behavior

Special attention was given to mobile sections such as navigation, accordions, typography, and hero content so the layout remains readable at smaller widths.

### Brand consistency

Visual refinements included:

- Laitco logo integration
- Branded loading/intro experience
- Custom favicon
- Consistent site title
- Technology-oriented hero imagery
- Cohesive dark / modern visual language

## Engineering & Design Challenges

### 1. RTL quality across responsive layouts

Layouts that work on desktop can become difficult to read on mobile when RTL spacing, icon placement, and content wrapping are not handled carefully.

### 2. Preserving brand quality in production

A polished design can degrade after deployment through incorrect asset paths, fonts, favicon configuration, or SPA routing. Production QA therefore became part of the implementation work.

### 3. Balancing visual richness and usability

Technology-oriented landing pages can become visually overloaded. The site was iteratively refined to keep its visual identity while preserving readable business messaging.

### 4. Persian-only presentation

English fragments in Persian interfaces can visually fragment the experience. Content and UI copy were reviewed to keep the public-facing site coherent for its target audience.
