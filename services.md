# Development Services

I build business applications and web products with Django, React and Next.js, from domain modeling and APIs to Persian RTL interfaces and operational reporting.

## Business systems

Accounting, inventory, sales, CRM and school-management workflows: structured records, operational forms, reporting and clear separation between domain services and presentation.

**Examples:** [Local Accounting](showcase/local-accounting/README.md), [Cafe Accounting](showcase/cafe-accounting/README.md), [School LMS](showcase/lms-real/README.md).

## Full-stack web applications

Django REST APIs, React / Next.js interfaces, typed clients, reusable form components and responsive management screens. Persian typography, RTL workflows, Jalali dates and localized amounts are represented in the source samples.

**Examples:** [NewCRM](showcase/newcrm-real/README.md), [Kerman Cars](showcase/kerman-cars/README.md).

## Workflow automation and reporting

Data-entry workflows, customer follow-ups, import-review interfaces, sales dashboards, inventory screens and operational notifications, with explicit validation and recoverable user actions.

**Examples:** [CRM engineering cases](showcase/crm-real/README.md), [ERP domain patterns](showcase/erp-accounting-real/README.md).

## Applied AI and document interfaces

Prototypes and tools combining structured inputs, image/document processing and human review. I distinguish exploratory capabilities from production integrations.

**Examples:** [Smart Home AI](showcase/smart-home-ai/README.md), [Automatic UI Evaluation](projects/automatic-ui-evaluation.md).
